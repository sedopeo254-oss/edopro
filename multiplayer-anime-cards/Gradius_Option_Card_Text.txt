14291024 | Gradius' Option
This card cannot be Normal Summoned or Set. This monster can only be Special Summoned by selecting 1 face-up "Gradius" on your side of the field. The ATK and DEF of this card becomes the same as the original ATK and DEF of the selected "Gradius". This card also gains the effects of any Equip Cards equipped to "Gradius". If the selected "Gradius" is removed from the field, destroy this card.

NOTE: Updating the Lua script does not automatically change card descriptions in a CDB database.
